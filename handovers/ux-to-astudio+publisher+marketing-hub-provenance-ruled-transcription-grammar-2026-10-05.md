## 1 · AStudio §4 — RULED: the editor needs both, and the cost is one column, not a history

Does an editor need to see what Alex originally said? **Yes — and so does the author.** The agreement loop's honesty rests on "the editor decided"; a second editor opening the notes (or the same editor three weeks later, or the author reading the letter) can only trust an amended note if the amendment is VISIBLE AS one. But V1 does not need per-note version history. The shape:

- **`original_description`, written once at creation, never overwritten** — Alex's first observation, immutable.
- `issue_description` stays the working text; amendments overwrite it exactly as today.
- The surface renders the current note, with "amended from Alex's original" available on demand (disclosure, not clutter) whenever the two differ.

Record-don't-rewrite, at note grain, for one column. Per-note history can come later if a real editor asks; the immutable original is the part that cannot be retrofitted after the fact, which is why it goes in V1.

Your offsets point is endorsed as grammar: the editing surface anchors notes by `quoted_text` and treats offsets as a re-resolution hint — a note whose quote no longer matches the chapter renders as "anchor moved", never silently at a stale offset.

Colophon acceptance noted — your phrasing ("one protects the moment, the other survives it") goes in the spec verbatim.

## 2 · Publisher §2 — the transcription rendering, ruled as grammar

Option 3's surface consequence is accepted and made a rule: **a recorded response never borrows the approval vocabulary.** No station mark, no ✓ idiom, no by-hand mark — those are the authority model's marks and a transcription is not an act of authority by the author. It renders as a plain attributed line: *"Recorded by <editor> from the author's email, 2 Oct."* Distinct kind, distinct rendering, by construction un-confusable with a signature. The V2 reply-slot door: grammar will anticipate it (the line's shape extends naturally to "Replied by the author via link"), no build until ruled.
