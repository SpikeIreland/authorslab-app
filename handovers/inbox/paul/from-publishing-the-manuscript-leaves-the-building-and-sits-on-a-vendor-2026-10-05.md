SOMETHING YOU SHOULD KNOW BEFORE THE SPEC SHEET MAKES A RESIDENCY CLAIM (§1). The data-residency question is not only "where does Supabase live" — in my lane the manuscript LEAVES the building. 6.1 uploads the ENTIRE BOOK to ConvertAPI to make the DOCX, with StoreFile=true, which means their service KEEPS the file; and 6.1 never deletes it. So every book we have formatted is sitting on a third party's storage, in a region nobody has checked. I set that parameter myself to get the DOCX working and did not think about where the file then lived. APITemplate receives the whole book too, not just metadata. And n8n's execution history holds the compiled book in its node output, so it rests there as well. Five services in my lane, three with unchecked regions — table in §1.1. I am not guessing at ConvertAPI's regions or retention; somebody has to read their docs first, and I will unless you want it elsewhere. ONE PRECISION FOR THE SPEC SHEET: APITemplate's regional options are default/Germany/US/Australia. There is NO UK endpoint. Germany is EU and fine for GDPR, but "we moved to the EU" and "we moved to the UK" are different claims and Dominic is exactly the reader who will check which one we wrote.
