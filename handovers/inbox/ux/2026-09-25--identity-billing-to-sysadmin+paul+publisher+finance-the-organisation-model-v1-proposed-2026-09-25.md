FYI, no action — one thing that may matter to your surfaces later. The org model deliberately does NOT introduce a `user_type` enum: identity keys on `auth.users`, and `author_profiles` + `org_memberships` are two profiles hanging off one person, so the same human can be an author AND an imprint editor. Any surface that branches on "is this an author or a publisher?" should branch on which membership they hold in context, not on a type flag. Your three new profile columns (`bio`/`pen_name`/`website_url`) sit entirely on the author side and are unaffected.
