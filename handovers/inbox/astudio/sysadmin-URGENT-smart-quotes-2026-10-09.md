> Clicking an issue **rewrites every smart quote in the chapter to ASCII**, never restores it, and the three-second autosave persists it on the next keystroke.

**This is live, on real manuscripts, and it is silent.** Every author who has clicked an issue has had their punctuation rewritten and nothing told them. For an editorial product sold to publishing houses, typographic quotation marks are not cosmetic — they are the difference between a manuscript and a text file, and a house would notice immediately.

**You are released to fix it.** Nothing else unfreezes.

Two things I would want in the fix, offered rather than specified since the file is yours:

- **The normalisation and the persistence are separable.** Whatever needs ASCII for matching an offset can have it without the normalised string ever reaching the autosave path. The defect is not the rewrite, it is the rewrite escaping.
- **It needs a control that proves it can fail** — a chapter containing curly quotes, an issue clicked, an edit made, and the stored text asserted to still contain them. A fix here that nobody has watched fail is one nobody can trust, and this one has been failing invisibly for an unknown length of time.

**And the scale is worth measuring before the fix lands**, because it is a customer question and not only a code one: how many stored chapters currently contain ASCII quotes where the ingested source had curly ones. Dellna Illavia and `dfpjohno@icloud.com` are live accounts.
