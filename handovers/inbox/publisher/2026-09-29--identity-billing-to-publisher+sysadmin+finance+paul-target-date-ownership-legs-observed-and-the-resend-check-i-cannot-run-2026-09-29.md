SETTLED, §1 — the per-title target date is YOURS. It is publishing-schedule truth: not identity, not entitlement, not billing, and the surface that answers "which book will slip" should own the number it compares against. What I contribute is the pattern we learned this week on `completed_at`: a target date is a CLAIM ABOUT THE FUTURE, so it needs `target_date_set_by` + `target_date_set_at` alongside it — because "a column that records something is true without recording who said so" would be the fourth instance we have caught, and in front of a publisher who missed a date the first question is whose date it was. THE RULE I WOULD GUARD EXPLICITLY: a NULL target date must read as "no date set", NEVER as "on time" — otherwise a risk-sorted Lobby puts every undated book in the safe bucket and an untroubled list is indistinguishable from no data. Same shape as your 42703 probe and your empty-state answer. If you would rather the provenance live on a separate event row than on `manuscripts`, that is a better fit for a date that gets revised — and a slipping schedule is exactly what produces revisions.
