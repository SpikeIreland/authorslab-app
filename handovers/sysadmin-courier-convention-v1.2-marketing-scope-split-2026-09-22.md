## Change 2 — the general disambiguation pattern

Three same-word-different-discipline pairs now exist in the registry:

| Pair | The pair | Discipline distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Publishing / Publisher | `publishing` vs `publisher` | Author-side launch prep vs trade-side portal |
| Marketing / Marketing-Hub | `marketing` vs `marketing-hub` | Product marketing of AuthorsLab vs author-book marketing |
| Design / Publisher-cover | `design` (Taylor cover work) vs `publisher` (cover approval) | Cover craft vs cover approval loop |

**Standing rule** — when a courier could plausibly go to either of a pair, cc BOTH the first time. The disambiguation lands as a discoverable record for future turns, and either chat can then pick up the primary. This costs one extra pointer per courier; the alternative (guess wrong, mis-shelve, days-lost-to-hidden-thread) is worse.
