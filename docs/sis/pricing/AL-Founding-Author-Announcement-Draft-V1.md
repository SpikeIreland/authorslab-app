**Subject: You're a Founding Author — and what that means**

Hi {first name},

When you joined AuthorsLab, you took a chance on something unfinished. You wrote
with our editors while we were still building the room around you, told us what
was broken, and shaped what this platform has become. That mattered more than
you probably realise, and we don't want it to pass unmarked.

So, first, the news: AuthorsLab is moving from the single package you bought to
a membership — one subscription that covers every book you write, with your
full editorial team at every stage, from Alex's first read to your launch plan.
New pricing goes live on the site in the coming days.

None of it costs you anything you've already paid for. **Everything you bought
is honoured in full** — your manuscript, your journey, every document on your
shelf, exactly as promised.

And because you were here first, you become a **Founding Author**. That means
the Author membership — unlimited projects, your whole editorial team — at
**£9.50 a month, for as long as your subscription stays active**. That's half
the standard rate, and it's not a promotional window: it holds for as long as
you stay. It's our way of saying the people who built this with us shouldn't
ever pay full price for it.

There's nothing you need to do today. Your Founding Author rate will be waiting
when you're ready, and I'll follow up with the simple step to claim it once the
new plans are live. If you have any questions — about the change, your library,
anything — just reply. It's me reading, as ever.

You'll also notice the platform looks different lately: your library, your
book's own room, the same editors in better light. I hope it feels like the
product you always suspected we were building. It exists in no small part
because of you.

With real gratitude,

Paul
Co-founder, AuthorsLab
