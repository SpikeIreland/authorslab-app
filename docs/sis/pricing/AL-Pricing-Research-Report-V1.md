## 1 · Headline answer

**£19/month (~$24) sits squarely mid-market** for individual writers: between
Sudowrite's Hobby ($10–19/mo) and Professional ($22–29/mo) tiers, ~2.3–3% of
a working UK/indie author's median income, and well inside the ~$81+/month
even low-earning indies report spending on marketing. But the writer market
is **bimodal and price-sensitive at the bottom** (44% of surveyed indies earn
≤$100/month from books; median all-author book income $2,000/yr US, £7,000 UK
and falling), so a single price point is wrong — a low entry tier and a
clearly higher-value professional tier are both warranted, and the imprint
segment is a different SKU entirely.

## 3 · Willingness to pay (all HIGH, primary surveys)

- US: full-time authors median $10k book income ($20k all-source); ALL
  authors median $2k book / $5k total (Authors Guild 2023, n=5,699).
- UK: primary-occupation authors median **£7,000**, down 38% real-terms since
  2018 (ALCS/CREATe 2022). £228/yr ≈ 3% of that income — material.
- Spend exists where commitment exists: successful indies ($10k+/mo) pay
  $250–$1,999/book for editing; average indie marketing spend $636/mo (~$81
  even among sub-$100 earners; self-selected samples, likely overstated).
- ALLi: committed indies' median gross ~$12,755 (2022), out-earning
  traditionally published peers.

**Reading:** £19/mo is low-to-mid for a committed indie, high for a hobbyist.
Price the commitment, not the average.

## 4 · Model shape (MEDIUM — one VC playbook + live competitor practice)

Bessemer's AI Pricing Playbook (Feb 2026): hybrid — base subscription for
predictability + usage/credit metering for expensive operations — wins when
cost/value dynamics are uncertain. Sudowrite and PublishDrive both run this
live. Cautionary tale in sources: Cursor's 2025 pricing change burned loyal
users by moving the meter without grandfathering — whatever AL chooses,
transition handling for the existing 11 beta authors matters.
**Fit with our estate:** the lmo_ledger (live 2026-07-27) will meter actual
per-author, per-station COGS within weeks of Craft Call rollout — the
credit allowances can be set from measured cost, not guesswork, and the
financial model's COGS lines become instrument readings. Investor-grade.

## 6 · Stress-test architecture (SYNTHESIS — validate with Van Westendorp on own audience)

- **Starter** ~£9–12/mo: 1 active project, capped analyses. (Hobbyist entry;
  bimodal-market answer.)
- **Author** ~£19–24/mo: multi-project, monthly deep-analysis credits, full
  publishing/marketing tools. Flagship — at/just above Sudowrite Pro to
  signal broader scope. *(Paul's £19 instinct lands here — validated as
  in-range, likely at the lower edge given AL spans 5 journey stages vs
  Sudowrite's drafting-only.)*
- **Pro** ~£39–49/mo: unlimited projects, rollover credits, priority.
- **Imprint SKU** £250–£750/mo: list-based (titles/year, Hederis pattern) or
  seats-with-minimum (Consonance pattern). Separate sales motion — the Jacky
  conversation prices against Consonance's £600/mo floor, not writer tiers.
- **Keep a one-time per-book pass** (~$149–299) as marketplace-style entry
  feeding subscription conversion — the current product becomes the funnel,
  not a casualty.
