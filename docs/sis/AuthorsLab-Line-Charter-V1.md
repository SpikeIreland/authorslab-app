# AuthorsLab — Line Charter V1

**FROZEN 2026-07-22** · S0 exit artifact · Spike Island Studios ⇄ AuthorsLab
Constituted by three documents: **this charter** · **AL-PFD-000 Rev C** (the
drawing) · **AuthorsLab-Client-Decisions.md** (D1–D9). Changes from here are
new numbered decisions producing new drawing revisions — never silent redraws.

## 1 · What the plant is
A production plant that takes an author from idea or manuscript to a
published, marketed book — personal and private by design (D5), owned by an
entity that may be a single writer or a small imprint (D7).

## 2 · Finished goods
1. **Edited manuscript** — editor-passed, author-approved per chapter (D6).
2. **Draft manuscript from an idea** — ghostwritten (Ivy/Reid), enters editing
   on the author's approval (D3).
3. **Treatment + scene breakdown** — from the edited manuscript; screenplay
   format is a later station (D8/D1).
4. **Design package** — cover (author-selected), front/back matter, interior
   formats.
5. **Publishing package** — metadata, ISBN, pricing, platform-ready files
   (KDP · IngramSpark · Apple; list may broaden, D4).
6. **Marketing campaign content** — author-approved before it speaks as them;
   platform integrations staged (D2, R2 open).

## 3 · The lines (7) and docks (3)
Lines: Ghostwriter · Manuscript Intake · Author Studio (3 phases,
author-in-the-carousel) · Script Adaptation · Design · Publishing · Marketing.
Docks: HOME (Companion chat → "make this a project") · IDEA (Eden Q&A) ·
UPLOAD (PDF/DOCX). Fork at project creation: Write → Ghostwriter; Edit →
Intake.

## 4 · People and ownership
The author is a station type — 7 inspection desks, three inside carousels.
Accounts are entities (person or imprint); members hold roles; **the project
owner holds the approval key at every desk** (D9). Tier-3 agency views:
deferred, roadmap. Migration-cheap rule binds all new tables, APIs, and copy
(per Platform Priorities Brief).

## 5 · Typing at freeze
14 AI (stochastic) stations · 6 deterministic · 7 human desks · 10 QC gates
(1 built, 9 grammar-required). The design law: no Craft station without a
gate at its next boundary.

## 6 · Anchors and constraints
- **September 2026 demo — Jacky Klein** (tier-2 imprint, Blair-backed; the
  R3 "partner" resolved as this customer). Demo build priorities P1–P5 per
  the Platform Priorities Brief; posture: AI-assisted, not AI-generated.
- Brownfield: live estate with real authors (11) and manuscripts (8);
  production_control live (10 sensors, historian); RLS enforced platform-wide.
- Data class: authors' unpublished manuscripts — highest-care customer IP.

## 7 · Open at freeze
**R2 only** — marketing integration scope & timing (staged per D2; revisit
after market research).

has typed stations; ownership defined. **S1 (I/O Schedule) is open.
