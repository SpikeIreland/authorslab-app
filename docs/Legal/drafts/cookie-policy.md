## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember things - whether you're logged in, your preferences, or how you arrived. Some cookies last only as long as your browser session; others persist on your device for a set period.

We also use similar technologies including local storage (to keep you logged in) and pixel tags (small invisible images that confirm whether an email has been opened).

### 2.4 Third-party cookies

When third-party services are loaded on our pages, they may set their own cookies. The main third parties whose services may result in cookies being set are:

- **Cloudflare** - used for DNS and security. Cloudflare may set `__cf_bm` (bot management) and `cf_clearance` cookies.
- **Stripe** - sets cookies described in 2.1 above when you visit payment-related pages.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels for retargeting, or social media embeds that set third-party advertising cookies.

## 3. What we don't do with cookies

- We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles.
- We do not sell or share cookie data with advertising networks.
- We do not use cookies to track you across other websites.

## 4. How to manage cookies

### 4.1 Browser settings

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

- see what cookies are stored
- block cookies from specific sites
- block third-party cookies generally
- delete all cookies when you close the browser

Links to cookie settings for common browsers:

- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/delete-and-manage-cookies)

Note: blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from logging in to AuthorsLab.

### 4.2 Our cookie banner

When you first visit `authorslab.ai`, we present a cookie notice that gives you control over non-essential cookies. You can update your preferences at any time via the cookie settings link in the site footer.
